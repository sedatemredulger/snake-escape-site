Privacy Policy

Snake Knot · Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy covers the Snake Knot: Escape Puzzle mobile game (previously published as Snake Escape; same app, same package name com.dukagames.snakeescape). The publisher and data controller is Düka Games (Sedat Emre Dülger, Ankara, Türkiye).

In short

  • No account required. We do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings are stored on your device only and never sent to us.
  • The game plays offline; the internet is needed for ads and usage measurement.
  • We do not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No in-app purchases.
  • We measure how the game is played, not who plays it.

Stored on your device

The following is kept in your device’s own storage and is not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

  • The level you have reached and the stars earned per level
  • Which tutorial steps you have seen
  • A simple counter used to pace ad frequency
  • Your language, sound, vibration and theme preferences

Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhat for
Internet · network stateLoading ads. The game itself runs offline.
VibrationTouch feedback. Can be turned off in settings.
App-specific random identifierUsage measurement. Not linked to your identity; removed when you uninstall the app.
Advertising ID (AD_ID)So Google AdMob can serve ads and avoid showing you the same ad repeatedly.

Ads

Snake Knot shows full-screen ads between levels, never in the middle of a level, and never less than ninety seconds apart. Extra hints, undos and continues can be earned by choosing to watch a rewarded ad; that choice is always yours.

Data that reaches third parties

We collect nothing ourselves; we run no server. When an ad is requested, Google AdMob collects its own data:

DataCollected byPurpose
Advertising ID (AAID)Google AdMobAd selection and frequency capping
IP address and the country inferred from itGoogle AdMobRegion-appropriate ads
Device model, OS versionGoogle AdMobFitting the ad to the device

How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/privacy

Because the app is distributed through Google Play, Google Play Services may also collect crash and stability data at the operating-system level. We have no access to that data and it is not gathered by any code we added.

Usage measurement

To improve the game we use Google Firebase Analytics. What is collected relates only to how the game is played:

What is recordedWhat it answers
A level starting, and which attempt it isHow many tries a level takes
A level ending: won or lost, time, number of moves, starsWhether levels are too hard or too easy
A level being left unfinishedWhere players give up
Use of hints and undoWhether the helpers are used on genuinely hard levels
When an ad was shown, and whether a rewarded ad was completedWhether ads appear too often

This is tied to a random app-specific identifier, not to your identity. No text you type, no location, and no access to your contacts or files is involved. Measurement starts only after ad consent is given; without consent, no event is sent.

The game contains no in-app purchases, subscriptions or crash-reporting library of our own. It makes no network requests other than ad requests and usage measurement.

Children

The content is suitable for all ages, but the app is published on Google Play for an audience of 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families programme. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we hold data about your child, write to us and we will verify and delete it.

Consent in Europe

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google’s consent screen appears before personalised ads and usage measurement. Without consent, measurement never starts. To change your choice later, clear the app’s storage in Android settings; the consent screen will appear again the next time you open the game.

Your rights

You can remove all game data from your device by uninstalling the app or choosing “Clear storage” in Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.

If you are in the European Union (GDPR) or Türkiye (KVKK) you have the right to access, correct and erase your data. Since we hold no record of you, such requests are usually directed to Google; write to us anyway and we will point you the right way.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy the date above changes.
