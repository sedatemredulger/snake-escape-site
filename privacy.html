Privacy Policy

Snake Escape · Last updated: 30 August 2026

This policy covers the Snake Escape mobile game. The publisher and data controller is Duka Games (Sedat Emre Dulger, Ankara, Turkiye).

In short

  • No account is required. We do not collect your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress (level, stars, settings) is stored on your device only.
  • The game runs offline; no connection is needed except to load ads.
  • We do not request location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.

Stored on your device

The following is kept in your device's own storage. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

  • The level you have reached and the stars earned per level
  • Your language, sound, vibration and theme preferences
  • A record of the remove-ads purchase, if you made one

Children

The content is suitable for all ages, but the app is published on Google Play for a target audience of 13 and over and is not part of the Google Play Families programme.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe we have, write to us at the address below and we will verify and delete it promptly.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — used to serve the in-game ads. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — for in-app purchases. Your card details never reach us.
  • Google Play Services — for crash reporting and distribution.

How long data is kept

Data stored on your device remains until you uninstall the app. Crash reports and anonymous usage counters are retained for Google's own retention periods.

Your rights

You can remove all game data from your device by uninstalling the app or by choosing "Clear storage" in Android settings. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalised ads in Android settings. If you are in the European Union (GDPR) or in Türkiye (KVKK) you have the right to access, correct and request deletion of your data; simply write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy the date on this page changes. If the change is significant we will say so inside the app.
